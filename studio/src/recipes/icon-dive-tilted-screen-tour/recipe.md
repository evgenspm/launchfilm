# Icon dive into a tilted dashboard tour

A three-line offer rises over a dark backdrop with a blue rim, an app-icon tile drops in and the camera dives through it, then seven light UI screens glide past on tilted planes under blue light, each held about 4 s.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/Hute1Vww (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A business or back-office product with many dense screens and no single hero moment.
- A silent premium showcase (site header, sales deck, social feed) where the screens carry the message.
- You have 5-7 clean screens and want each one readable for about 4 s.
- The offer (book a demo, free trial) should open the film instead of closing it.
Not for: products with one killer interaction (nothing in the UI animates here), or films that need a closing CTA or end card (the reference ends on a screen and a black frame).

## Look
Palette: #000201 background black, #F2F4F4 UI plate white, #0A1C3B deep blue light, #3B5074 mid blue glow, #989D9F UI grey text, #2A2E31 vignette grey. Type: offer in condensed bold sans, title case, tight, 3 centred lines ~6.5 % H each; UI uses small tracked uppercase labels and bold italic screen titles. Treatment: glow, vignette (heavy, every screen row); far edge of each plane in depth-of-field blur. Background: flat black with blue light shapes (rim, streaks, bands) behind the planes.

## What to adapt
- Copy: one opening statement, 3 lines of 3-4 words, saying the outcome and how to get a demo. No other copy; the UI carries the rest.
- Brand: the accent blue drives the rim, streaks and glow; the mark appears once, on the app-icon tile in row 2. Add an end card after row 13 if you need a CTA (the reference has none).
- Timing: screen rows hold 3.3-4.7 s each; keep them ≥ 3 s so dense tables stay readable. Rows 1-2 (4.23 s) keep their frame counts: the offer hold and the 24 fr dive.

## Pitfalls
- Camera speeds are low and constant (1-3 px/fr, 1-6 %/s). Faster moves make the small UI text unreadable.
- The UI never animates; adding cursors or count-ups changes the genre from showcase to demo.
- Without the vignette and edge blur the light plates on black look flat and cheap; the far edge of each plane must fall off.
- Vary the plane angle per screen (leaned back, steep, face-on, 40 deg diagonal, slab): seven identical tilts read as one long shot.

## Missing components
- PlaneSheen: a soft white or blue light band that sweeps across a TiltPlane surface over 15-30 fr, angle 30-45 deg, opacity ≤ 0.35 (techniques: glow). TiltPlane has `sweep?`; verify it covers this before building a new one.
- IconTileDive: rounded app-icon plate drops in from above on springs.ui, then ZoomThrough scales it past the frame in ~15 fr (techniques: zoom-through). Composable from ZoomThrough + a tile; no single preset.

## Proposed terms
- plane-sheen: a light band sweeping across a tilted UI plane to add life with no UI animation (rows 4, 6, 8). Not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json.
- icon-dive: camera dives through an app-icon tile into the first screen (row 2). Covered by zoom-through in recipe.json; listed for a more precise id.

# Macro phone study-app tour, scan to quiz

A dark phone UI shot in shallow-focus macro on tilted planes: home, new topic, scan pages, a topic card counting up, step trail, reading screen, mascot and a quiz answered. Black tagline and wordmark close.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/HeFudGqz (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A mobile app with a clear multi-step flow (create, capture, learn, test) to walk through in about 30 s
- Dark product UI that looks premium when lit like a physical object in macro
- An app with a mascot or character that can carry an emotional beat mid-film
- Store launch where the close is mark, wordmark and store badges
Not for: light UIs (the macro depth look needs dark screens and a dark blue void), products with a single screen, VO-led explainers.

## Look
Palette: #090A0B background, #E2E4E5 UI text, #4C5F92 accent blue (buttons, backdrop glow), #242D40 dark blue void, #2B3835 progress green (dark), #7B5B5B warm mascot shadow. Type: geometric sans UI, sentence case, list titles ~5 % H in macro; close: bold geometric wordmark ~4 % H and a small regular tagline ~2 % H, white on pure black. Treatment: vignette. Background: dark navy radial glow behind the phone, pure black for the close.

## What to adapt
- Copy: one tagline (~7 words) on black; UI copy is the app's own (screen headers, button labels, 3-5 list items with progress).
- Brand: the accent blue carries primary buttons and the backdrop glow; progress uses green; the logo appears in the hook UI and the final lockup with icon and store badges.
- Timing: rows 3, 6 and 8 can stretch; the cuts at rows 4, 9, 16 and the 37 fr black before the tagline keep their frame counts.

## Pitfalls
- Shallow focus is the look: only a narrow band (~20 % H) is sharp in macro rows; with deep focus the UI reads as a flat screenshot.
- Real cuts are only three; everything else is camera travel inside one shot. Do not add cuts between rows marked continuous.
- The close is abrupt and flat (no camera, no glow): keep it, the contrast with the moving macro rows is the punctuation.

## Missing components

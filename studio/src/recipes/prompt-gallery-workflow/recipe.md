# Prompt gallery proof reel

A typed request opens a vertical reel, a dense gallery and rolling count prove breadth, one example becomes a branching workflow, and a compact mark-and-URL close converts the proof into action.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/8cQzMX1c (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-07 · status: built

## When to use
- A vertical developer-tool promo needs a fast proof of breadth.
- A product can show many visual examples before one focused workflow.
- A voice-over needs clear visual chapters without a music bed.
Not for: products with fewer than a dozen strong visuals or no single breadth figure.

## Format
9:16 · 14.12 s · 30 fps · light · no music, BPM unavailable, cut_on_beat 0/1 · VO yes, 24 words at about 1.83 words/s over the spoken span

## Look
Palette: pale blue-white background `#F2F6F5`, near-black text `#010201`, white cards `#FEFEFE`, muted text `#575858`, sage `#D8D9C7`, muted rose `#A69497`. Type: bold grotesk, sentence case, tight tracking, 4–9 % H, with narrow serif and script accents. Treatment: none. Background: flat pale field carrying a wall of colourful owned examples in the proof section.

## What to adapt
- Copy: 6 short lines total: prompt ≤ 6 words, hook and bridge 4 words each, two proof labels, 5 workflow words, then two compact close lines.
- Brand: use owned examples, the brand mark, and neutral workflow glyphs; keep the pale field and reserve mixed display faces for one emphasis word and the lockup.
- Timing: preserve the 1.27 s count entrance, 2.5 s proof hold, 1.73 s focused example, and final 0.85 s URL hold.

## Pitfalls
- A sparse gallery breaks the breadth proof; 12–16 distinct examples must remain legible as a texture at 720×1280.
- The single detected cut is at 3.73 s. Moving it disconnects the spoken hook from the proof reveal.
- The 8.27–10.00 s focus move combines a fast zoom and 961 px normalized pan; extending the exit beyond 9 fr stalls the handoff.
- Do not reproduce third-party marks in the workflow tiles; use neutral glyphs or user-owned assets.

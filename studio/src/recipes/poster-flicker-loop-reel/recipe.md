# Poster flicker montage loop

About 28 full-bleed vertical posters hard-cut every 2 to 14 frames (4 cuts per second), with 2 to 3 frame interstitial frames, slice glitches and a white flash as accents; it ends on the opening poster so it loops.

Source: https://jitter.video/template/drop-it-like-it-s-hot-sizzle-reel/ (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-07 · status: studied

## When to use
- A studio or designer showreel built from finished poster, cover or campaign stills
- A launch-day vertical teaser where the volume of work is the message
- A seamless loop for a social profile, story or event screen
- Silent autoplay feeds: nothing has to be read
Not for: a single product with one claim, anything that needs copy to be read (no poster holds longer than 14 fr), viewers sensitive to flicker.

## Format
9:16 · 6.17 s · 30 fps · mixed · music none (silent reference), cut_on_beat n/a · VO no

## Look
Palette: #000000 background (black posters), #F6F6F6 white posters and type, #FD48AE hot pink accent, #4E36D7 violet, #FD0403 red, #85B4D3 pale blue. Type: poster typography owned by each still, heavy condensed or wide grotesk caps, tight tracking, 10–25 % of frame height, cropped at the frame edges; the edit adds no type. Treatment: grain (in the stills). Background: full-bleed stills, no shared backdrop.

## What to adapt
- Copy: none added by the edit; all type lives inside the user's posters. Readability is not the goal.
- Brand: alternate dark, light and one saturated accent colour every 1–3 posters; the strongest colour poster sits around 1 s (row 5); the logo can be the opening and closing poster.
- Timing: keep holds between 2 and 14 fr and the average near 7 fr; keep the 2–3 fr interstitials; rows 19–20 must stay the fastest stretch before the loop frame.

## Pitfalls
- Holds over 14 fr kill the flicker; the longest hold measured is 14 fr (row 11).
- Order by contrast: two neighbours with the same background read as one shot.
- The last poster must equal frame 0 exactly, or the loop seam shows.
- About 4 cuts per second: add a flicker warning where a platform requires one.

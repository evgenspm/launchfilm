# Archive photo montage into blue-glow UI tour

Fast black-and-white office photos set up the struggle, a typed line on black turns the corner, then a blue-lit product tour: laptop push-in, multiplayer editor, a glowing button flipping in edge-on, a post, URL, mark.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/ZTbPvi98 (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A founder or startup-program launch that opens on the hard, human side before showing the tool
- A video or design tool with collaboration (named cursors) and an export step to show
- A silent autoplay film where one strong colour (electric blue) carries the product half
- Contrasting a grey past with a lit present in one film
Not for: products with no UI to rebuild, or brands without a mark that holds up full-frame.

## Look
Palette: #000000 background, #D2E1ED near-white text and screen highlights, #3E9BFC glow accent, #1D4CC3 deep blue, #0C266B navy glow falloff, #BFBFBF photo mid-grey (archive half). Type: medium-weight grotesk, sentence case, normal tracking, statements ~3 % H centred; button label bold ~8 % H. Treatment: glow. Background: flat black with blue radial glows in the product half; monochrome photos in the opening.

## What to adapt
- Copy: wordmark + one 4-word claim (built word by word), one 2-word line, one action word on the button, one 4-word closing phrase, one URL with a path.
- Brand: the accent colour is the glow; it must be absent from the first 6.2 s. Mark appears full-frame at 14.73, in the lockup at 16.00 and alone at the end.
- Timing: the photo montage keeps its 3–10 fr shots; rows 4–11 can stretch ±20 %; the end mark keeps 60 fr.

## Pitfalls
- Colour enters only at 6.20: tinting the photos breaks the past/present contrast.
- The button turn reads only if it starts truly edge-on (a sliver) and the glow is visible before the face is.
- The flash is 2 fr; longer flashes swallow the mark reveal.
- Rows 4–11 have no cut: every handoff is a camera move or match-cut, so keep the camera always moving.

## Missing components
- EdgeFlip: a flat glowing UI element (button/card) rotates on Y from 90° to 0° with perspective 1200 px, glass sheen and bloom, 54 fr, drop-in 15 fr from above (techniques: bloom, glow).
- PhotoMontage: list of photos, per-photo frame counts (3–10 fr), inset size 55–75 % W, monochrome filter (techniques: none in vocabulary).

## Proposed terms
- edge-on-flip: element enters seen edge-on and rotates to face camera, row 6 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)
- mono-archive: black-and-white archival photo montage as the "before" half, rows 1–2 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

# Wallpaper prompt tour with lens wipes and monitor close

A one-word mark types over flower footage, a ring opens onto a desktop wallpaper where a prompt is typed and sent, then a desk with a capability scroll, a glass-lens headline over autumn footage and a monitor end card.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/9HAbMEbj (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- An AI assistant or productivity tool that wants a friendly, nostalgic look instead of dark UI
- Showing one prompt being asked, then many things the tool covers
- A 30 s social cut that ends on a URL and a logo on a device
- Brands with a mascot or small mark that can sit alone on screen
Not for: dark, technical brands or films that need real product UI beyond one prompt bar; products without a short wordmark.

## Look
Palette: #F3F3F3 studio white (end card bg), #353737 dark text on the monitor, #2586F3 wallpaper sky blue and send-button accent, #D1835B tulip orange, #B66918 autumn amber, #BFA380 desk wood. Type: rounded geometric sans, bold, sentence case, white on footage at ~7 % H for headlines; caps label for the name; dark on white at ~4 % H on the monitor. Treatment: liquid-glass (lens rim and bubble, pill button). Background: stock footage / photos, four scenes, then a flat white studio.

## What to adapt
- Copy: name (1 word), category (1 word), question (~6 words), prompt (~6 words), 10–14 capability names (1–3 words), action label (1 word), headline 8 words split 4 + 4, 4 action labels (3–4 words), CTA 2 lines (invitation + URL), slogan 2 × 2 words.
- Brand: accent on the send button and the rising circle; mark tile and wordmark only on the monitor at the end.
- Timing: rows 5, 8 and 12 stretch with copy length; keep the iris (18 fr), lens grow (11 fr), flash (4 fr) and white wash (10 fr).

## Pitfalls
- The film has only 3 hard cuts; everything else is a soft wipe or continuous, so do not add cuts between scenes.
- The capability list is readable only for one row; keep the others dimmed or they compete.
- The send circle has to cross the hard cut at 9.57 in the same screen position, or the bridge to the desk breaks.
- Footage-based look: if the user has no background footage, swap in BrandBackdrop and drop the nostalgia angle.

## Missing components
- IrisLens: circular ring mask that grows from a point to full frame over 11–18 fr, with a refracting glass rim (techniques: mask-wipe, liquid-glass)
- DotSatellites: four small dots at top/left/right/bottom of a word, spacing follows the word width on springs.ui (techniques: typewriter)
- MonitorFrame: front-on desktop monitor with stand, screen ~55 % W, rises from below (techniques: end-card)

# Multiplayer cursor mockup collage

A one-word-at-a-time sentence on white while named cursor tags draw frames that fill with device mockups; the collage collapses to a sphere, a dark mark beat, staged phone shots, a gallery that scatters and stacks, then a quiet end card.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/mRI6PLFk (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A design or mockup tool that wants to show many polished outputs in 30 s
- Products with real-time collaboration where named cursors are part of the story
- A silent autoplay launch on a light canvas with one dark brand interlude
- You have 10 or more finished renders or screenshots to show as a gallery
Not for: products without strong visual output (a backend, an API); films that need a spoken or written argument, since copy is limited to 1-2 small words per beat.

## Look
Palette: #FFFFFF canvas, #070908 type, #0130C1 accent blue (gradient, cursor tag, button), #4576E4 light blue, #02061B dark interlude base, #EBE8DE warm off-white in mockups. Type: medium grotesk, sentence case and lowercase, about 4 % of frame height, centred. Treatment: mesh-gradient, duotone, glow, vignette, grain, motion-blur. Background: flat white for the canvas rows; animated mesh gradient for the mark beat; per-mockup blurred colour backdrops for rows 10–13.

## What to adapt
- Copy: a 4-step sentence of 1–2 words per step (rows 1–3), a one-word button label, a 3-word closing line, the URL. Nothing longer.
- Brand: the accent blue carries the mesh gradient, the first cursor tag, the selection frames and the button; mark appears in rows 6, 13 and 19.
- Mockups: 10–13 finished renders; the two phone renders for rows 7–8 must share a pose so the screen swap reads as one object.
- Timing: rows 1–5 and 17 stretch with copy; rows 10–13 must keep 14–15 fr each; row 20 holds ≥ 90 fr.

## Pitfalls
- Type is never large: scaling the words up turns the film into kinetic type and buries the mockups.
- Rows 10–13 are 14–15 fr shots; longer holds break the burst between the two calm halves.
- The rock-and-phone stills are pre-rendered; do not attempt them in 3D.
- Cursor tags must carry real-looking names and three distinct colours, otherwise the collaboration idea is lost.

## Missing components
- CursorTag: arrow plus coloured name pill that drags a thin 1 px selection frame whose corners follow the arrow, springs.ui 12 fr (techniques: cursor-path)
- SliderPanel: small dark rounded panel, label plus one slider, value driven by a cursor drag (techniques: cursor-path, ui-rebuild)
- CollageCollapse: N cards converge to centre, shuffle z-order every 7 fr, fold into a sphere that shades from multicolour to navy (techniques: converge)

## Proposed terms
- collaborator-cursor-tag: a named, coloured cursor pill that draws a selection frame which then fills with content; rows 1, 2, 7, 8 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)
- collage-to-sphere: a converged card stack folds into a shaded sphere that becomes the wipe shape into the next scene; rows 4, 5 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

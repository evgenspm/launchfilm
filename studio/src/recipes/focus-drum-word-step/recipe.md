# Focus drum word stepper

A tilted column of single words on black steps up one line every 0.75 s. Only the word at the arrow is sharp and white; neighbours fall off into grey blur, so the eye locks on one word at a time and the list reads as momentum.

Source: https://jitter.video/template/blur-text-scroller/ (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-07 · status: studied

## When to use
- Title or chapter intro listing 6-12 qualities or themes, one word each
- Silent loop for a hero banner or social header
- Opener before a product statement, naming what the product is about
- Editorial or portfolio reels where type is the whole picture
Not for: copy that needs sentences or a claim to be read in full; each entry must be one or two words.

## Format
16:9 · 9.00 s · 30 fps · dark · music none (reference is silent), cut_on_beat n/a (0 cuts, no beats) · VO no · loopable

## Look
Palette: #000000 background, #F6F6F6 active word and arrow, #7B7B7B and #5B5B5B near neighbours (grey by distance), #252526 far blurred words. Type: bold geometric grotesk, sentence case, tight tracking, active word ~14 % H, line pitch ~20 % H. Treatment: none. Background: flat black.

## What to adapt
- Copy: 12 entries of 1-2 words (the reference uses one two-word entry); themes or qualities of the product, all the same grammatical kind so the rhythm reads.
- Brand: active word in brand fg (or accent for the arrow only), neighbours in muted; flat brand bg. No logo in the reference; add an end card after the loop if needed.
- Timing: every step is 22-23 fr (0.75 s); keep it even. Fewer words shortens the loop (n × 0.75 s); the wrap must land on word 1 so the loop is seamless.

## Pitfalls
- Steps alternate 22/23 fr (0.73/0.77 s in the sheet timestamps); a drifting period breaks the metronome feel.
- Only one slot is sharp; if the neighbours are too sharp the focus effect disappears, too blurred and the list stops reading as a list.
- The tilt is on the whole column, not per word; rotating words individually looks broken.
- Keep the arrow fixed in place: only the words move.

## Missing components
- FocusDrum: vertical list on a shallow curve, one active slot sharp at full fg, blur and opacity growing with line distance (about 0.06 em blur, -25 % luminance per line), steps one line per period with easings.outExpo 8 fr, wraps, column rotated -5 deg, optional marker glyph at the active slot (techniques: stacked-roll, blur-in). StackedRoll is a rolling drum with constant speed and no focus slot, so it covers only part of this.

## Proposed terms
- focus-drum: word drum that steps line by line, one sharp active slot, depth blur and dim by distance; rows 1-13 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

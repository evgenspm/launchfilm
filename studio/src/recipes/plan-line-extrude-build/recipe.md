# Point to line to plan to built place

A single dot stretches into a line, the line draws a floor plan, the plan tilts into 3D and extrudes into lit volumes inside a growing site, with figure callouts and two short statements, then rewinds to the line and the wordmark.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/31WSJcqS (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- Architecture, real estate, construction or spatial-design products that turn drawings into places
- A build-from-nothing story told in one continuous camera move, no cuts
- A slow premium loop for a site hero, showroom or event screen
- Showing 3-4 project figures over a hero object without any UI
Not for: products with no physical or spatial object to build; fast feeds where 30 s per cycle is too slow.

## Look
Palette: #000000 background, #DAD9CF wordmark and statements, #B8B5A6 cream volumes, #848475 tan rule and light walls, #4F554B sage glass and streets, #2C2C2C base slab. Warm amber interior light (rows 6-7) is a small accent. Type: thin geometric sans, all caps, wide tracking, wordmark ~8 % H over a hairline warm rule; captions and statements small tracked caps ~2 % H; figures light sans ~6 % H with tiny caps labels. Treatment: none (flat shading, soft directional light, no grain). Background: flat black.

## What to adapt
- Copy: wordmark (1 word), tagline (1 short line), side caption (2 lines, ≤ 5 words, the idea that everything starts as a line), two statements (3 words then 2 words: what it is not, then what it is), 4 figures with one-word labels (count, area, floors, phase).
- Brand: accent only on the hairline rule under the wordmark and the warm interior light; the model stays in neutral cream/sage.
- Timing: one cycle (rows 1-11, 31 s) is a complete film; rows 12-20 are a repeat plus a variant. Stretch the figure and site holds (rows 7, 9), keep the build steps one cell (~8 fr) apart.

## Pitfalls
- No cuts at all: every beat hands over by continuous change; adding cuts breaks the drawing-becomes-place logic.
- Layers must arrive in architectural order (plan, columns, walls, solids, glazing, light, site); out of order reads as random.
- Statements and figures sit in the empty space above the building; the pull-out in row 8 makes that room, so keep it before the statements.
- Second wordmark (row 12) is larger than the first; keep one size if you run a single cycle.

## Missing components
- PlanDraw: SVG floor plan whose strokes draw on in sequence (stroke-dashoffset), rooms close, inner walls follow; ~2 s for 15-20 strokes (techniques: logo-from-dot)
- ExtrudeBuild: R3F scene that tilts a flat plan from top-down to ~40 deg elevation and extrudes rooms in layers (columns, translucent walls, solids, glazing, interior lights), each layer ~8-10 fr apart, and can play in reverse (techniques: orbit-3d)
- SiteGrow: outline streets and plots around the model that fill solid and extrude neighbour blocks with a left-to-right wipe over ~0.75 s (techniques: orbit-3d)

## Proposed terms
- plan-draw: strokes draw a floor plan from one line (rows 3, 13) (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)
- extrude-build: a flat plan rises into 3D volumes layer by layer (rows 5-6, 14, 20)
- build-rewind: the finished build strips back to the plan and line in reverse order (rows 10, 18)
- site-grow: context (streets, neighbour blocks) grows around the hero object (rows 8-9, 16-17)
- stat-callout: figures appear in fixed slots over a 3D object and swap two at a time (rows 7, 15)

# Desk laptop opener into a voiced editor step tutorial

A filmed laptop on a dark desk shows a finished phone render, then the film becomes a screen capture on a blue backdrop that walks through each editor step under VO. One continuous shot, the result proves the steps.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/d0bwPGAN (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A creative tool shows how fast a result is made, step by step, with VO
- Vertical social tutorial where the finished output must open the film
- A real screen recording exists and each step is one or two clicks
- Showing that settings (zoom, tilt, animation, effects, export) change a preview live
Not for: products without a visual output to open on; silent autoplay feeds (the VO carries the steps, there are no captions).

## What to adapt
- Copy: no on-screen copy; the VO script is 11 short phrases (~10 words each), one step per phrase: result promise, then each step, then the export.
- Brand: the blue gradient backdrop takes the brand accent; the tool UI itself carries no extra branding moment (no logo end card).
- Timing: rows 2–12 stretch with the VO phrase lengths; row 1 (4.75 s opener) and row 13 (~1 s click hold) keep their lengths.

## Pitfalls
- No cuts at all: every step change is a camera move on the capture, so jump-cutting between steps breaks the format.
- The opener shows the finished result first; starting on the empty canvas loses the hook.
- Effects rows (10) blur the whole preview: keep the blur on the device only, the camera move must stay readable.

# Horizon glow claims, photo fly-through and podium

A 60 s voice-led pitch on black with a blue horizon glow: phone peek, one-word claims, a fly-through wall of frustrated photo tiles, then one feature per beat (button press, card grid, podium count, tilted phone) and a typed URL lockup.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/cJpg69yX (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A 60 s voice-over explainer for a marketplace, wallet or payments app
- The story needs a clear pain beat (old way) before the product appears
- Several features, each one short line plus one simple visual, no footage needed
- Dark brand with one cool accent colour that can carry the horizon glow
Not for: short silent loops, or products with one hero claim (the film is a list of features carried by VO).

## Format
16:9 · 60.65 s · 30 fps · dark · music 69.9 BPM, cut_on_beat 3/3 · VO yes, 107 words over ~59 s (~1.8 words/s)

## Look
Palette: #000000 background, #DAE9F3 text and glow core, #7197EE accent glow, #10161E and #27334D dark gradient steps, #B4DAFD glow highlight. Type: bold grotesk, sentence case, white, claim words ~7 % H, feature lines ~5 % H. Treatment: glow (horizon and panel rim), halftone (hands, row 12). Background: black top fading into a blue horizon glow along the bottom third, constant through the film except the photo-wall panel.

## What to adapt
- Copy: 1 title (2 lines), 3 opening claims (1–2 words), 1 intro line, 1 button label, 1 heading, 6–8 feature lines (≤ 8 words), 3 verbs, URL. Every line follows a VO line.
- Brand: the accent colour drives the horizon glow and the panel rim; mark appears in rows 7 and 18 only.
- Timing: rows follow the VO; the 3 hard cuts sit on beats and should stay on beats. Feature rows stretch with their VO line; keep entrances at 0.25 s.

## Pitfalls
- Only 3 cuts in 60 s: everything else is state change in one shot on the same glow. Adding cuts breaks the calm.
- The photo wall works because the camera never stops; a still wall reads as a collage, not a crowd.
- Each feature needs one visual idea; rows with only text (13, 17) are short or single words.

## Missing components
- RimPanel: inset dark rounded panel (radius ~3 % W) with a 2 % W glowing accent rim, holding a scene (techniques: depth-field, glow)
- MedalPodium: three ranked medals hanging from the top edge (order 3-1-2, centre 30 % larger), each with a Counter below (techniques: counter)
- HeldTokens: row of 5 halftone hand cut-outs holding images that flip on a 0.5 s cycle (techniques: halftone)

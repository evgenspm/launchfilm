# Glass mark macro orbit, light-on and dive

Macro glimpses of a blue glass mark, then a thin glowing slab drifting through black; it turns face-on, goes dark, lights up with a wide halo, the camera dives through it into a glowing wordmark and a tagline.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/49CnmevG (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A logo teaser or brand intro where the mark itself is the story (no UI, no claims).
- A dark cinematic opener before a launch film: keep rows 8-12 (light-on, dive, name) as a 6-8 s sting.
- A brand whose mark is a simple geometric shape that reads as one extruded solid.
- Silent autoplay: suspense comes from light, not from sound.
Not for: brands with a detailed or multi-colour mark, light themes, or films that need the product shown in the first 10 s.

## Look
Palette: #000315 background black-navy, #9B9F84 text grey-white, #63D2FE lit cyan core, #1C5DF7 brand blue body, #06309F deep blue halo, #0F35A4 mid blue. Type: medium geometric sans, sentence case, normal tracking, about 4 % of frame height, centred, soft white glow. Treatment: glow. Background: flat black with a radial blue halo field behind the mark.

## What to adapt
- Copy: 2 lines: the product name (1 word, gets a period), then a tagline of about 4 words that wipes on beside it.
- Brand: one brand blue carries body and halo; only the lit core turns cyan (or a lighter tint of the accent). The mark appears in rows 1–11, the name in 12–16.
- Timing: rows 1–6 stretch or drop freely (a launch-film cut keeps 23.77–31.50); the light-on (14 fr core, 5 fr halo lag), the 29 fr dive and the 5 fr black must keep their frame counts.

## Pitfalls
- The mark is revealed by light, not motion: it never bounces or overshoots. Any spring with overshoot breaks the premium feel.
- Only the core goes cyan; the halo stays pure blue and wide (two radii). A cyan halo looks cheap.
- The dive must accelerate on ln(scale) (inExpo); a linear zoom feels like a slideshow.
- Skip the 5 fr black breath and the name lands on top of the dive.
- 38 s of macro is long for a feed; for a launch film compress rows 1–7 to 2–3 s.

## Missing components
- GlassSolid3D: an extruded translucent mark with facets, camera orbit and a travelling specular patch (2-4 % W wide, 250-300 px/s, cyan, about 10 px bloom); GlassMark fakes it in 2.5D (techniques: glass-mark, orbit-3d).
- LightWindowSweep: a blue highlight window travelling along a line of text over about 20 fr, word by word (techniques: light-on).
- TypeWipeOn: a line wiping on left to right while the existing words re-centre on springs.layout (techniques: none in vocabulary).

## Proposed terms
- specular-sweep: a hot highlight travelling across a nearly still object so it reads as glass (rows 1–2) (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

- light-window-sweep: a light band travelling along words of a settled line (row 13) (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

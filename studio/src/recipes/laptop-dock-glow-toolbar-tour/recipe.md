# Laptop dock push into a glowing toolbar tour

A lit laptop pushes in on one hopping dock tile and match-cuts to the mark; a glowing pill toolbar, word-by-word claims on black and drag-to-select grabs that drop into an editor canvas sell a screen-grab tool.

Source: https://www.raylight.app/templates/z9u6bWLc (link only; the original is not redistributed) · studied 2026-10-08 · status: studied

## When to use
- A screen or screen-grab tool whose UI is a small floating toolbar
- A desktop utility launched from a dock or menu bar
- Showing grabs becoming editable layers in a second app
- Silent social cut where short black statements carry the pitch
Not for: products with no compact floating UI, or films that need VO and music carried by the edit.

## Format
16:9 · 30 s · 30 fps · dark · music none (silent reference), cut_on_beat n/a (0 beats) · VO no

## Look
Palette: #000000 background, #FFFFFF type, #133987 accent blue (wallpaper, rim glow), #0D2146 navy (mark backdrop, gradients), #86AECE light streak, #566AA5 muted blue. Type: bold geometric grotesk, sentence case, ~8 % H statements, small bold URL. Treatment: glow (blue rim around the toolbar, streaked wallpaper). Background: flat black for type, blue gradient behind screenshots.

## What to adapt
- Copy: 4 statements of 2–3 words (toolbar name, footage, no export step, price), 3 single source nouns, a 4-word layer line, a 3-word sign-off, URL.
- Brand: accent carries the wallpaper streaks, the toolbar rim glow and the selection rectangles; the mark appears as dock tile, full-frame, and alone before the URL.
- Timing: the dock push (120 fr) and the logo hold (105 fr) stretch; word-by-word statements keep ~8 fr per word.

## Pitfalls
- The match-cut only works if the dock tile and the full-frame mark share the same shape and centre.
- Most real scene changes sit on black and were not detected as cuts; rebuild them as hard cuts.
- The opening push must ramp: a constant push reads slow and the match-cut loses its punch.

## Missing components
- DockRow: frosted rounded shelf with N app tiles, one tile hops (springs.snappy) (techniques: device-laptop, ui-rebuild)
- GlowToolbar: dark pill bar with icon+label buttons and a 2 px accent rim plus ~40 px outer glow, hover/selected states (techniques: glow, ui-rebuild)
- SelectionMarquee: accent-outlined translucent rectangle drawn from cursor drag start to end (techniques: cursor-path)

## Proposed terms

- name-writes-mark-out: the name types in from the left while the mark is pushed out on the right, row 3 (not in craft/vocabulary.md; not used in recipe.json)

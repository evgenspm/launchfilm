# Showcase

Films people made with launchfilm. Opt-in only: nobody's film appears here unless they add it.

## Add yours

Open a pull request that adds one row to the table below. Keep it to facts.

- **Film**: a public link (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, your site). No uploads to this repo.
- **Product**: name and URL.
- **Made by**: your name or handle.
- **Presets**: which platform presets you rendered (`x-16x9`, `li-4x5`, `li-1x1`, `ph-16x9`, `hero-loop`, `reel-9x16`).
- **Recipes**: recipe ids you started from, if any (from `recipes/index.json`).
- **Agent**: the coding agent you used.
- **Note** (optional, ≤ 120 characters): what you changed, what worked, what didn't.

By adding a row you confirm the film is yours to share and that its music and footage are licensed for it.

# Hook bank

A hook is frame 0 (the poster) plus the first change by frame 15 (0.5 s @30) plus the category being clear by 1.5 s
(1 s on reel-9x16). Muted autoplay is the default on X, LinkedIn and reels, so the hook must work without sound.
Example lines are for fictional products; write your own for the user's product.

| ID | Pattern | Frame 0 (poster) | Change by fr 15 | Mode fit | Acme example | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Friction in frame | the painful state of the user's work: 14 unpaid invoices in red | the count ticks 14 → 15 | both | "Fifteen invoices. Nobody paid." | wallowing: move to the turn by 4 s |
| H2 | The break | a clean, calm UI | it cracks, scatters or glitches apart | launch film | "Your inbox was fine at nine." | gimmick if the payoff doesn't fix what broke |
| H3 | One line in, everything out | an empty prompt or field with a blinking caret | typing starts; result begins to build | product film | typed: "invoice Dana for March" | slow typing kills it: ≤ 2 fr/char |
| H4 | The number | one large, sourced number on the brand bg | the number counts or a unit lands | both | "3 hours a week. On reminders." | must be real and cited in BRIEF.md |
| H5 | Before → after | split frame or wipe, "before" half visible | the wipe crosses the frame | both | messy spreadsheet → one clean ledger row | two focal points: lead the eye with the wipe |
| H6 | Claim card | one 5–7 word statement, hard cut in at fr 0 | second statement or the dim-emphasis on the key word | launch film | "Invoices should chase themselves." | reads as an ad if the claim is generic |
| H7 | Direct question | the question as hero type | the answer word lands | launch film | "Who reminds the client?" | rhetorical questions with obvious answers fail |
| H8 | Impossible camera | an extreme detail (one cell, one pixel, one key) | fast pull-out reveals scale | both | one "Paid" badge → pull out to 400 paid rows | needs a payoff for the scale |
| H9 | Mid-action product | the product UI already in motion, cursor mid-path | the click lands | product film | cursor arrives on "Send reminders" | needs a readable UI at 1080p: punch in |
| H10 | The ticker | a clock, counter or calendar running fast | it stops on the moment that matters | both | calendar flips to the 30th, overdue badge drops in | cliché if the stop has no meaning |
| H11 | The meta catalog | "Every launch video has…" style list, self-aware | first trope appears and gets crossed out | launch film | "Every invoicing app shows a dashboard. We won't." | needs a genre the audience knows |
| H12 | Sound-first, text-carried | black frame with one word set large (never empty) | a hard sound + the second word | launch film | "Ping." … "Ping." … "Paid." | must still read muted: the words carry it |
| H13 | The user's own words | a real quote from a user or the founder (permission!) | the next line or the product answer | both | "I spend Fridays chasing money." | no permission → don't |
| H14 | The object | the product's physical or iconic object in light (device, card, mark) | light-on or a rotation reveals it | launch film | the invoice PDF folding into a paper plane | slow: keep under 1.5 s before the first word |

## Checks for any hook

- [ ] Frame 0 renders as a still that works as a thumbnail at 480×270 (text ≥ 6 % of frame height).
- [ ] A visible change by frame 15.
- [ ] Category clear by 1.5 s with sound off.
- [ ] The promise is paid by the payoff beat.
- [ ] No banned phrase `[craft/11#banned-phrases]`.

Cite as `hook H3 [craft/01#hooks]` in STORYBOARD.md.

# Structures by length

Seconds and frames at 30 fps (at 60 fps double the frames). Beat labels only from the vocabulary:
`hook · problem · tension · reveal · feature · proof · social-proof · cta · logo · outro`.
Adjust any section by ≤ 20 % and keep the order. At 120 BPM a bar is 2 s (60 fr): the 6/30/45/60 s boundaries below
sit on even seconds, so sections start on bars `[craft/02#placement-on-the-grid]`. Full law: `craft/01#structures`.

## 6 s bumper (hero teaser, reel opener)

| Section | s | fr | % | beat | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–1 | 0–30 | 17 | hook | frame 0 = poster; one change by fr 15 |
| Proof | 1–4.5 | 30–135 | 58 | proof | the one moment the product does its thing |
| Logo + URL | 4.5–6 | 135–180 | 25 | logo | mark settles (heavy spring), URL types or appears |
No VO or ≤ 8 words. 1–3 shots.

## 15 s product film (single feature, 0–2 cuts)

| Section | s | fr | % | beat | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Establish | 0–1.5 | 0–45 | 10 | hook | UI at tier 1 (0.80–0.90), empty or friction state |
| User acts | 1.5–4 | 45–120 | 17 | feature | typing, cursor, one action at tier 2 |
| Product works | 4–11 | 120–330 | 47 | reveal | staggered results, tiers 2→3, one tier per move |
| Hold result | 11–14 | 330–420 | 20 | proof | still readable, drift only; ≥ 3 s |
| Out | 14–15 | 420–450 | 6 | logo | pull-out to tier 1 or cut to the mark |

## 90 s founder story

| Section | s | fr | % | beat | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| World | 0–27 | 0–810 | 30 | hook (0–3), tension | how it used to be or should be; warm, slow, 3–4 s shots |
| Problem escalates | 27–41 | 810–1230 | 16 | problem | colder, 0.5–1 s shots, faster cuts |
| Break | 41–45 | 1230–1350 | 4 | tension | crash, glitch or silence; rhythm break |
| Pain | 45–51 | 1350–1530 | 7 | problem | one long shot (up to 5 s) of the cost |
| Answer | 51–75 | 1530–2250 | 27 | reveal, feature, proof | the product, the making-of, calm pace |
| Promise + logo | 75–90 | 2250–2700 | 17 | cta, logo | quiet close on black, 3–4 s, then the mark |
